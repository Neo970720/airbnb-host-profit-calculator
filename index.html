3% versus 15.5% is a price game

Under split fee the guest's total sits 14.1%–16.5% above your nightly rate, so your own price — and the 3% taken from it — is smaller. At a 15% guest fee the two structures land within 46 cents a night of each other. Below a 14.79% guest fee, split fee pays more.

The cleaning fee is taxed like room revenue

A $150 cleaning fee nets $126.75 after the host-only fee. If the cleaner charges $110, that turnover earned you $16.75 — not $40. A one-night stay can lose money while looking like a booking.

Break-even occupancy is the survival line

Fixed bills ÷ what each booked night leaves you. If a 2-bedroom needs 11 nights a month and you average 8, the year is a loss no matter how good the reviews are. The first ten points above that line are your profit.
